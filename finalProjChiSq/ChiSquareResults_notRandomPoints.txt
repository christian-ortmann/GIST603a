Chi Square Analysis Results

Null Hypothesis: The points in nests are randomly distributed in the veg raster.

Point Features
	Feature Class Name: nests
	Total Number of Points (n) = 50

Raster
	Raster Name: veg
	Total Number of Classes = 7

## Chi-Square Statistic

Observed Chi-Square = 65.5405

Degrees of Freedom = (7 - 1) x (2 - 1) = 6

Theoretical Chi-Square for P = 0.05 = 12.59

Observed Chi-Square > Theoretical Chi-Square

We reject the null hypothesis
Points in the nests feature class are NOT randomly distributed in the veg raster

## Cramer V Statistic

V = 0.8096

The classes in the veg raster strongly influence the distribution of points in the nests feature class.
